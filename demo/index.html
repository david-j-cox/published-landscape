Published Landscape

Behavior-analysis journal articles are published and browsed chronologically, which makes it hard for editors to find who has written on a given topic beyond the names they already know. This organizes articles from the last 10 years by topic instead, so readers can find articles by theme and associate editors can find reviewers by expertise.
